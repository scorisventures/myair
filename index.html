        Phone access

        This phone reaches your aircon through the internet (via ntfy.sh), at home and away. While this page is open it updates about once a minute, and straight away after each change. If the internet at home is down, use the wall panel.

        Reminder sounds

        Reminders and camera alerts still show during quiet hours. These settings are kept on this tablet. If no sound plays, tap the screen once after the page loads, or turn on Autoplay audio in Fully Kiosk.

        Screen dimming

        The screen goes dark with a clock when nobody has touched it for a while. A tap, a new reminder or a camera alert brings it back (after a reminder or alert it stays bright for 30 minutes). Quiet hours are set under Reminder sounds. Keep the tablet's screen on with Android's Stay awake (Developer options). Kept on this tablet.

        NTFY Me on this tablet

        Kept on this tablet. The camera topics are in the landlord settings.

        Room sensors

        Bluetooth thermometers for rooms without an Advantage Air sensor. While the aircon heats or cools, the controller holds each linked room at its set temperature by changing its airflow. Match a sensor by the temperature on its screen.
